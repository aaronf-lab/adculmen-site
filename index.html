The founder

He learned how insurers and lenders judge a building. Then he built that view for the people who take care of buildings.

Before founding Forensic Roofing Sciences, Aaron M. Forsman worked for State Farm, Allstate and Aflac, and then as an independent agent. That work taught him how insurers and lenders look at a building: the dates they ask for, the documentation they expect, and how quickly a claim weakens when the record is missing.

He also spent years on roofs after storms. Owners, managers and boards were rarely the ones holding that record, even though they were the ones paying for the roof. So he built it himself, one building at a time, until the method was dependable enough to repeat. That became AD CULMEN™.

AD CULMEN™ gives facility teams, owners and boards the same clear view of a building's storm history that insurers and lenders rely on.

“The storm isn’t history. It’s inventory.”

The name is Latin, roughly “to the summit.” The roof is where the evidence lands first, and where it disappears fastest.
