These terms are between Forensic Roofing Sciences LLC, Saint Paul, Minnesota, and the buyer named on the order.

1. What you receive

  • A list of properties inside a radar-estimated hail swath for the storm and counties you order. Each record carries the owner of record, the owner's mailing address, the property address, county and parcel ID, the radar hail band at the property, the storm date and the property type.
  • The mail tier contains no telephone numbers and no email addresses.

2. License

  • Forensic Roofing Sciences LLC ("FRS") grants you a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the list for your own business's marketing by U.S. mail for 12 months from delivery.

3. What you may not do

  • Resell, sublicense, rent, share, publish or give the list (or any part of it) to anyone else, including affiliates, vendors or other contractors, except a mail house that mails it only for you.
  • Combine it into a list or database offered to others.
  • Use it to decide credit, insurance, employment, housing or any other eligibility.
  • Append phone numbers or emails and contact owners by phone, text or email unless you independently comply with every law that applies to that contact.

4. Your compliance

  • Run CASS address matching and NCOA (USPS Move Update, DMM 602) before mailing at presort or automation prices.
  • Follow federal and Minnesota mail, advertising and telemarketing law, including the TCPA (47 U.S.C. § 227), the Telemarketing Sales Rule (16 C.F.R. Part 310) and Minnesota's telephone solicitation laws.
  • Minnesota residential roofers, contractors and remodelers may not advertise or promise to pay or rebate any part of an insurance deductible, or offer anything of value for an inspection, a claim or a referral (Minn. Stat. § 325E.66). Your mail must comply.
  • Public adjusters must follow Minn. Stat. ch. 72B, including its limits on initiating contact.
  • Hold every license your work requires (for example, a Minnesota residential roofer license).

5. Accuracy and sources

  • Records come from public county parcel data (MetroGIS / Minnesota Geospatial Commons) and NOAA/NSSL MRMS radar data. The list is provided "as is." A hail band is a radar estimate of hail size at the property; it does not state that any roof is damaged.
  • Public data publishers' disclaimers apply to you as well as to FRS.

6. Owners who opt out

  • If an owner asks not to be contacted, stop contacting them and tell FRS at aaron@forensicroofingsciences.com so we remove them from future lists.

7. Non-exclusive

  • The same records may be licensed to other buyers. FRS licenses a record to no more than three buyers per storm.

8. Price, delivery and refunds

  • Prices are shown on the order page. Lists are delivered by email within 24 hours of payment. Because lists are digital, sales are final, except that FRS will credit any delivered record missing a mailing address.

9. Misuse

  • FRS may include seed records to detect misuse. If you breach these terms, the license ends and FRS may pursue its remedies.

10. Liability

  • FRS's total liability is limited to the amount you paid for the list. FRS is not liable for indirect or consequential damages. You are responsible for, and will indemnify FRS against claims arising from, your use of the list.

11. Law

  • Minnesota law governs. Venue is Ramsey County, Minnesota.

12. Acceptance

  • You accept these terms by checking the box on the order page, paying, or using the list.
