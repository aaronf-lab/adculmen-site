Likely questions

How is this different from a hail-history report?

We read NOAA radar hail size at the building’s own ~1 km cell, then require a second independent source (an NWS warning over the point or a ground report nearby). Radar alone is flagged, not counted. Every row is sourced and the method is written down.

How reliable is radar hail size?

MESH bands are floors, never a measured stone. That is why a day counts only when two sources agree, and why the record shows exposure, never damage.

Do you give engineering opinions?

No. We document weather and conditions. Structure, uplift, deck, façade and anything stamped go to Walter P Moore.

Do you work insurance claims?

We don’t adjust or negotiate claims. We provide a dated storm chronology with claim windows that your team or the owner can use.

Who is in the field?

Our team, to your written protocol: photos, coordinate-tagged observations, test cuts only where you direct. Fastest in our Upper Midwest core group; national with scheduling.

What about your roofing company?

Forsman Roofing Solutions is an affiliated company and is disclosed on every report. Clients are never required to use it. In a WPM engagement, WPM decides who performs any work.

Where would we overlap?

Only on roof condition assessment, handled case by case. Whoever brings the client holds the relationship.

How do you handle client data?

Mutual NDA first. Client addresses are used only for that assignment.

What does it cost?

We’d rather start by showing what we can do. The pilot is no cost, and we’ll fit how Walter P Moore engages partners.

Are the turnaround times guaranteed?

They are targets for discussion, set per assignment.
